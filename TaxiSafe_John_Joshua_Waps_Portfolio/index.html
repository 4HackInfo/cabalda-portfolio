PROJECT 001 ↗

TaxiSafe™

An Internet of Things-Based Taxi Driver Safety and Emergency Response System with Real-Time GPS Tracking and Risk-Weighted Predictive Routing in Cebu City

TaxiSafe is a proposed connected safety system designed to support taxi drivers during emergencies. The concept combines an IoT-enabled alert device, live location sharing, incident monitoring, and routing recommendations informed by road-risk data.
